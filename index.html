Self-managed super, minus the paperwork

SpudSuper is an Australian SMSF administration service for workers aged 25 to 55 who want more control over their retirement savings. We take care of the admin that comes with a self-managed super fund, from bookkeeping and investment tracking to annual financial statements, the SMSF annual return, audit coordination and compliance deadlines, so you can focus on your investment strategy.

  • Bookkeeping
  • Annual financial statements
  • SMSF tax returns
  • Audit coordination
  • Compliance tracking
